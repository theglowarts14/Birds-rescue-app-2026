## In one paragraph

**Karuna is the operating system for animal-rescue NGOs in India.** A free, open multi-tenant SaaS that replaces WhatsApp chaos with a 4-tap reporter app, a real-time coordinator dashboard, an auditable donor portal with auto-generated 80G receipts, and a roster system for volunteers and vets. Hyderabad first, with Animal Warriors Conservation Society as our design partner. The goal is to make every Indian city's rescue helpline as reliable as a 1098.

## The problem we are obsessed with

A black kite is hanging by a manja thread off a tree in Banjara Hills. A teenager spots her. He's standing on the road with a smartphone and twenty seconds of attention.

Today, this is what happens:
- He Googles "bird rescue Hyderabad," finds a WhatsApp number, sends a photo.
- The number is a coordinator's personal WhatsApp. She's at the vet with another case.
- He waits. The bird waits. Forty minutes pass.
- The coordinator responds. Asks for the area. Sends a volunteer.
- The volunteer goes to the wrong tree. Calls the teenager. The teenager has left.
- Sometimes the bird is saved. Often she isn't.

**The bottleneck is not goodwill.** AWCS has 60+ volunteers, three vets, a rehab center. The bottleneck is *coordination*. The bottleneck is *software*.

## What Karuna does

Three surfaces, one backend:

**1. Reporter app (native, free).** Four taps: kind → problem → urgency → photo + GPS. The case lands in the right team's queue in under two seconds. The reporter follows the rescue: en route → in care → released. She sees the bird she helped fly again.

**2. Team ops (web, multi-tenant).** Coordinators see today's cases by urgency, dispatch volunteers with one tap, write treatment notes, track recovery, mark releases. Auto-router sends cattle to the goshala, snakes to Friendicoes, borewells to the forest dept. Every status change is logged immutably for funders and auditors.

**3. Donor portal (web, white-label per NGO).** Donors don't see a "donate" button — they see a clay water bowl, a square metre of net, a specific bird in care. They get her photo updates until release. 80G receipts are auto-generated by Razorpay. Recurring monthly donors fund whoever needs it next.

All built on a single multi-tenant Postgres with row-level security: one Karuna serves many NGOs without their data ever touching.

## Why now

Three things converge:

1. **Smartphone penetration in tier-2 India crossed 70%.** Reporters can be reached natively.
2. **DPDP Act 2023 + 80G digitization** make NGO compliance painful enough that automation is bought, not built.
3. **Razorpay + Supabase + Expo** make it possible for a small team to build production multi-tenant infrastructure in months, not years.

We've waited. Now we can serve.

## Why us

- **Editorial design that NGOs cannot buy.** Most NGO software looks like 2014. Karuna is *The Verge* meets a vet clinic — Fraunces + Inter, paper + rust, every page reading like a magazine spread. Donors are visually literate. Trust is shaped by aesthetics.
- **Built with AWCS, not for them.** Co-authored schema. Weekly demos. Real cases tested every week.
- **Multi-tenant from day one.** Adding an NGO is a row in `organizations`. Not a fork. Not a deploy. Not a custom instance.
- **Open source.** Karuna's web + native + schema are MIT-licensed. NGOs can leave.
- **Free forever for verified NGOs.** Karuna's revenue (later) comes from optional services: white-glove onboarding, custom CSR portals, premium support. Never from the rescue itself.

## The ask

| Time horizon | Amount | What it funds |
|---|---|---|
| Bridge (M1–M2) | ₹4–6 lakhs | Cloud (Supabase, Vercel, Razorpay), one external pentest, AWCS staff time for testing |
| Phase 1 (M3–M6) | ₹15–25 lakhs | Single full-time founder salary, one designer 50%, second engineer for M5+, NGO #2 + #3 onboarding |
| Phase 2 (M7–M12) | ₹40–60 lakhs | Team to 4. WhatsApp BSP. Telugu + Hindi production. 8 NGOs live. |

**Total Y1 ask: ₹60–90 lakhs (≈ $72k–$108k USD).** Comparable nonprofit-tech projects (e.g. Glific) raised $1M+; we are asking for a fraction because Supabase + Expo + Razorpay collapse the infra cost.

## What the money produces

- A free public good that **every animal-rescue NGO in India can use the day after we ship.**
- A measured 60–70% reduction in time-to-dispatch for any NGO that adopts it.
- A new donor cohort: people who don't donate to "an NGO" but to "the bowl on a tree in Banjara Hills."
- Open source code + schema + design that other social-impact projects can fork.
- A repeatable playbook for "multi-tenant SaaS for Indian NGO sectors" — applicable to mental health, urban farming, elder care, refugee aid.

## Risks (and what we do about them)

| Risk | Our move |
|---|---|
| AWCS doesn't adopt | We've co-built, not pitched. Weekly demos. |
| Donor side doesn't convert | Test "give a thing" copy with real donors in M1. Pivot if needed. |
| RLS bug causes cross-org leak | External pentest in M2 before public donors arrive. CI tests. |
| Costs balloon | Supabase + Vercel + Razorpay free tiers cover us until ~10k MAU. Per-NGO usage cap in code. |
| Founder dependency | Phase 2 hires within first 4 months. Docs in `karuna.app/docs` from day one. |

## Where Karuna is in 5 years (the vision)

- **The rescue helpline of record** for every major Indian city.
- Federated with **forest department** + **municipal corporations** so officials see the same case dashboard.
- The default 80G + CSR reporting platform for **animal welfare** (and a template for adjacent sectors).
- A research substrate — every release is a data point on urban biodiversity, climate stress, manja patterns, festival risks.
- 1M+ rescues recorded, named, and traceable.
